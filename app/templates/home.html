How a {{ BRAND }} valuation works

  1. You enter the numbers

    Revenue, profit or MRR, age, growth and a few risk questions. Nothing is pulled from third parties, and nothing you enter is published.

  2. A base multiple is applied

    Profit-based businesses start from a multiple of annual profit (SDE). SaaS and AI businesses start from a multiple of ARR. Each range is a published platform assumption.

  3. Risk adjusts the multiple

    Age, growth, owner time, channel and customer concentration, churn and margins move the multiple up or down. Every adjustment is shown with its reason.
