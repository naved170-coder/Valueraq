## What this tool does

Newsletter valuation is the process of estimating the value of an email publication from the profit it earns, the loyalty of its readers and how dependent it is on its writer and sponsors. This tool applies a profit-multiple method to newsletters funded by sponsorships, paid subscriptions or both.

It's useful for writers thinking about selling, operators buying newsletters to build a media portfolio, and anyone comparing a newsletter listing's price with its numbers.

## Inputs you need

| Input | Why it matters | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Average monthly revenue and net profit | Profit is the base of the valuation | Yes |
| Subscribers | Recorded in your report for context | No |
| Average open rate (last 90 days) | Indicates how engaged the list is | No |
| Revenue model | Paid subscriptions are recurring and direct | No |
| Largest sponsor's share of revenue | One sponsor paying most of the bills is a risk | No |
| Growth, age and owner hours | Direction, durability and the work involved | No |

Open rates are imprecise. Apple Mail Privacy Protection, introduced in 2021, pre-loads emails and can register opens that never happened, which inflates reported rates. Click rates and replies are useful supporting evidence for buyers.

## What affects a newsletter's value

**Dependence on the writer.** Readers often subscribe for a person's voice. If the founder writes every issue and the newsletter carries their name, a buyer must convince readers to stay through the handover. Newsletters with a publication brand, several contributors or a format-driven product (curated links, data, job listings) transfer more easily.

**Sponsor relationships.** Repeat sponsors, a booked-ahead calendar and published rates are evidence that revenue will continue. Heavy reliance on one sponsor is a risk the tool adjusts for.

**List health.** Buyers look at growth sources (organic referrals versus paid acquisition), unsubscribe rates, and how many subscribers are active. A smaller engaged list can be worth more than a large inactive one.

**Platform portability.** Subscriber lists can usually be exported, but paid subscriptions may need customers to re-authorise payment on a new platform. Check what your platform allows before you agree terms.

## Limitations

- Pre-profit newsletters, and those sold for their audience to a strategic buyer, are outside this method.
- Deliverability, list consent records and data-protection compliance need separate checks.
- The base multiple is an editorial assumption. The estimate depends on your figures and is not an appraisal.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much is a newsletter worth per subscriber?

{{BRAND}} doesn't use a per-subscriber price, because subscribers of different newsletters earn very different amounts. Profit, engagement and how the newsletter earns money are better guides. Divide the tool's result by your subscriber count if you want to see the figure it implies.

### Are paid newsletters worth more than sponsored ones?

Under this model, paid subscriptions add a small positive adjustment because the revenue is recurring and comes directly from readers. A sponsored newsletter with strong, diverse sponsor demand can still be worth more overall.

### What if I write every issue myself?

Record your weekly hours in the form. The tool can't measure how attached readers are to you personally, so if the newsletter depends on your voice, expect buyers to ask for a transition period or an earn-out.
