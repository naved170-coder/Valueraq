## How the marketplace works

**Sellers** create a free account, submit a listing with their figures and a description, and wait for review. We check each listing against the [marketplace rules](/marketplace-rules/) before publishing it. Website addresses and contact details stay private.

**Buyers** browse by category, compare listings using the implied multiples shown on each page, and contact sellers through their account. Seller-provided figures are marked as unverified unless a listing shows a [verification status](/verification/).

{{BRAND}} doesn't handle payments between buyers and sellers, act as a broker, or hold funds. Buyers and sellers agree terms directly and should use a reputable escrow service and professional advice for the transaction.

## Before you buy

1. Check the asking price against the business's figures with the [Business Multiple Calculator](/calculators/business-multiple-calculator/) or the valuation tool for its type.
2. Read the guide to [buying digital businesses](/guides/buying-digital-businesses/).
3. Request evidence for every important figure, following the [due diligence guide](/guides/digital-business-due-diligence/).

## Before you sell

Value the business first with the matching [valuation tool](/tools/), gather 12 to 24 months of financial records, and read [how to sell a digital business](/guides/selling-digital-businesses/). Listings with complete figures and a clear, specific description pass review faster.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does it cost anything to list?

Listing is free. Sellers can optionally pay to feature a live listing at the top of its category; see [pricing](/pricing/).

### Are listing figures verified?

Not by default. Figures come from sellers. When our team has checked evidence such as payment-processor records or analytics access, the listing shows a verification status explaining what was checked.

### Why are some categories empty?

The marketplace only shows real businesses submitted by their owners. We don't add sample or placeholder listings, so categories fill as sellers join.
