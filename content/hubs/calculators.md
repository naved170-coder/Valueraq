## Calculators or tools?

Use a **calculator** when you already know the figure you need and want the arithmetic done, for example checking what "40× monthly profit" means in dollars, or what multiple a listing's asking price implies.

Use a **[valuation tool](/tools/)** when you want {{BRAND}} to estimate the multiple for you from the business's age, growth and risk factors. The tools take a couple of minutes longer and explain every adjustment.

Both use the same engine and the same published [methodology](/methodology/), so their results are consistent.

## Common questions the calculators answer

- What would my website be worth at 30, 36 or 48 months of profit?
- Roughly what is a SaaS business with $20,000 MRR worth?
- Is this $300,000 asking price high for a site earning $7,500 a month?
- How many years would it take for profit to repay the purchase price?

For definitions of the terms used, see the [glossary](/glossary/), in particular [revenue multiple](/glossary/revenue-multiple/), [profit multiple](/glossary/profit-multiple/) and [ARR](/glossary/arr/).
