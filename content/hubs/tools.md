title: Digital Business Valuation Tools
answer: VALUERAQ's valuation tools estimate what a digital business is worth from the figures you enter. Profit-based businesses (websites, apps, newsletters and ecommerce stores) are valued on a multiple of annual profit; SaaS and AI businesses on a multiple of annual recurring revenue. Each tool adjusts its multiple for age, growth and risk, and shows every step.
---

## Which tool should I use?

Choose the tool that matches how the business earns most of its money:

- **[Website Valuation Tool](/tools/website-valuation/):** content, affiliate, display-ad and lead-generation sites.
- **[SaaS Valuation Tool](/tools/saas-valuation/):** subscription software, including plugins and micro-SaaS.
- **[AI Business Valuation Tool](/tools/ai-business-valuation/):** products built on machine-learning models, such as AI SaaS and APIs.
- **[App Valuation Tool](/tools/app-valuation/):** iOS and Android apps.
- **[Newsletter Valuation Tool](/tools/newsletter-valuation/):** sponsored and paid email newsletters.
- **[Ecommerce Valuation Tool](/tools/ecommerce-valuation/):** online stores, marketplace sellers and DTC brands.

If a business mixes models, for example a content site that sells a subscription product, use the tool for the largest source of profit and note the rest.

## What every tool has in common

Every tool uses the same published [methodology](/methodology/):

1. It calculates a base metric from your figures: annual profit or ARR.
2. It applies a default base multiple range for the business type. These ranges are platform assumptions and are labelled as such.
3. It adjusts the multiple for each risk factor you answer, with the reason for each adjustment shown.
4. It returns a low, midpoint and high value.

Results label every number as user-provided, a platform assumption or calculated. Nothing you enter is stored unless you save a report to a free account, and saved reports are private.

## What the tools don't do

They don't verify your figures, connect to your accounts, or replace a professional appraisal. They also don't model deal structures such as earn-outs. Read the [limitations](/methodology/#limitations) before relying on an estimate.
