## How it works

Some costs rise with every sale, such as product cost and payment fees. Others stay the same however much you sell, such as a fixed software subscription or a salary. A business with a lot of fixed cost has **high operating leverage**: once sales cover the fixed costs, each extra sale adds a lot of profit. The same structure works in reverse when sales fall.

**Degree of operating leverage = contribution margin ÷ operating income**

Here, [contribution margin](/glossary/contribution-margin/) is revenue minus variable costs, and [operating income](/glossary/operating-income/) is what is left after fixed costs too. Multiply the result by a percentage change in sales to estimate the percentage change in operating income.

## Example

Two businesses each sell $100,000 a year and earn $20,000 of operating income. The figures are made up.

| | Business A (mostly fixed cost) | Business B (mostly variable cost) |
|---|---|---|
| Variable costs | 30% of sales: $30,000 | 70% of sales: $70,000 |
| Fixed costs | $50,000 | $10,000 |
| Operating income | $20,000 | $20,000 |
| Contribution margin | $70,000 | $30,000 |
| **Degree of operating leverage** | 70,000 ÷ 20,000 = **3.5** | 30,000 ÷ 20,000 = **1.5** |

Now sales rise 20% to $120,000:

- **A:** variable costs $36,000, fixed $50,000, so operating income is $34,000, up 70% (20% × 3.5).
- **B:** variable costs $84,000, fixed $10,000, so operating income is $26,000, up 30% (20% × 1.5).

If sales fall 20% to $80,000 instead, A earns $80,000 − $24,000 − $50,000 = $6,000 (down 70%), and B earns $80,000 − $56,000 − $10,000 = $14,000 (down 30%).

## Why it matters in a valuation

Buyers price [profit](/glossary/operating-income/), so they ask how steady it is. A business with high operating leverage can grow profit quickly, but a bad quarter hurts it more. When you present a business for sale, show which costs are fixed and which vary, so a buyer can judge both the upside and the risk. The [break-even point](/glossary/break-even-point/) tells you how much revenue is needed just to cover the fixed costs.
