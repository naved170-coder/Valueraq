## How it works

An owner who hires a broker hands over much of the work of selling. The details differ from one broker to the next and are set out in the agreement the owner signs, but the usual steps are:

1. **Preparing the sale**: gathering the financial records, agreeing an [asking price](/glossary/asking-price/) and writing the listing or sales summary.
2. **Finding buyers**: contacting people who may be interested and screening them, often by confirming they can fund the purchase.
3. **Managing the process**: handling questions, arranging calls, helping with a [letter of intent](/glossary/letter-of-intent/) and coordinating [due diligence](/glossary/due-diligence/).
4. **Closing**: working with the lawyers, the [escrow](/glossary/escrow/) provider and both sides until the sale completes.

A broker works for the seller in most arrangements. Check the agreement to see whose interests a particular broker represents.

## Example

An owner sells a content website for $300,000. The broker's agreement states a fee of 10% of the sale price, payable at closing.

**Fee = $300,000 × 10% = $30,000, so the owner receives $270,000 before other costs.**

The 10% is an invented figure used only to show the arithmetic. Real fees vary by broker and by deal size, and some brokers also charge an upfront fee. Always read the agreement for the rate, what it is applied to and how long it lasts after the contract ends.

## Why it matters when buying or selling

A broker can save an owner time and bring buyers the owner could not reach alone. That can matter for a larger business, or for an owner with no time to run a sale. The cost is the fee and, in some cases, less control over who sees the business.

A broker does not change what the business is worth. Price still follows earnings, trend and risk, and a broker can only help the owner reach buyers and negotiate. A free estimate from a [valuation tool](/tools/) can help you judge whether an asking price suggested by anyone is reasonable.

Brokers are one route among several. Compare them with listing on a [marketplace](/glossary/marketplace/) or selling directly to a buyer you already know in [ways to sell a digital business](/guides/ways-to-sell-a-digital-business/).
