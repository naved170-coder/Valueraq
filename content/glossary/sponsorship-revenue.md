## How it works

A sponsor pays for a place in front of your audience. In a newsletter, that is usually a slot in each issue. The price may be:

- **A flat fee per placement**, for example one slot in one issue.
- **A package**, such as four issues for one price.
- **A fee tied to results**, such as a payment per click. See [earnings per click](/glossary/earnings-per-click/).

Sponsorship differs from [display advertising](/glossary/display-advertising/), where an ad network fills the space automatically. With sponsorship, you or an agent sell each slot to a named business.

## Example

A newsletter has one sponsor slot per issue and sends four issues a month. It sells three of the four slots in an average month at $250 each. The figures are an illustration, not market prices.

**Monthly sponsorship revenue = 3 × $250 = $750.**

**Annual sponsorship revenue = $750 × 12 = $9,000.**

If one sponsor buys two of the three slots every month, that sponsor provides $500 of the $750, or 67% of the revenue. Losing that sponsor would cut income by two thirds.

## Why it matters in a valuation

Buyers look at whether sponsorship income repeats. Sold-out slots, sponsors who rebook and prices that hold steady are good signs. A few large sponsors, or one-off deals that do not renew, make the income less certain. This is a form of [customer concentration](/glossary/customer-concentration/).

Buyers also check that the audience supports the price. [Open rate](/glossary/open-rate/) and [click-through rate](/glossary/click-through-rate/) show whether readers pay attention to the placements, which is what sponsors are buying.

{{BRAND}}'s [newsletter valuation tool](/tools/newsletter-valuation/) values a newsletter at a multiple of annual profit, with a default range of 2.5 to 4.0 times that the [methodology](/methodology/) explains. Sponsorship revenue feeds that profit figure: use revenue actually received, minus the costs of finding and serving sponsors. The [newsletter valuation guide](/guides/newsletter-valuation/) goes through the full process.

## Related terms

[Open rate](/glossary/open-rate/) is the share of delivered emails that are opened. [Click-through rate](/glossary/click-through-rate/) is the share of readers who click. [Customer concentration](/glossary/customer-concentration/) describes how much revenue depends on a few buyers.
