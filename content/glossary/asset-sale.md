## How it works

The sale contract, often called an asset purchase agreement, lists what is being sold. For a digital business that usually includes:

- Domain names, websites and content.
- Source code, apps and app-store listings.
- Brand names, trademarks and social media accounts.
- Customer or subscriber lists, where the law and the privacy policy allow them to be transferred.
- Supplier, advertiser or customer contracts that can be assigned.
- Inventory, for an ecommerce business.

Anything not listed stays with the seller. That normally includes the seller's company, its bank accounts, its cash and its debts.

## Asset sale vs share sale

| | Asset sale | Share (stock) sale |
|---|---|---|
| What the buyer acquires | Listed assets | The company itself |
| Past liabilities | Generally stay with the seller, unless the contract says otherwise | Generally stay inside the company, which the buyer now owns |
| Contracts and accounts | Each one has to be transferred or re-signed | Stay in the company's name |
| Typical use | Most small digital businesses | Larger businesses with many contracts, staff or licences |

This table is a general description. What actually transfers depends on the contract and on local law.

## Example

A seller owns a company that runs two online stores and sells one of them. The buyer purchases that store's domain, storefront, product listings, supplier agreements and $20,000 of inventory. The seller's company, its other store and its bank loan are not part of the deal. The buyer sets up their own payment and advertising accounts, because those cannot simply be handed over.

## Why it matters when buying or selling

- **Buyers** usually prefer an asset sale because they can choose what they take on and are less exposed to problems from before the sale.
- **Sellers** need to plan the handover. Every account, licence and contract has to be moved one by one, and some platforms restrict transfers.
- **Price** can be affected. The two structures are often taxed differently for each side, so the same headline price can leave a different amount after tax.
- **[Due diligence](/glossary/due-diligence/)** should confirm that the seller really owns each asset on the list and has the right to transfer it.

Most listings of small websites, apps, newsletters and stores are sold as asset sales, with payment handled through [escrow](/glossary/escrow/).

## Take advice

The legal and tax effects of an asset sale differ by country and by business structure. Speak to a lawyer and an accountant before agreeing which structure to use.
