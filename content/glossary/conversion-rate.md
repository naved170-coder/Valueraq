## Formula

**Conversion rate = conversions ÷ visitors (or sessions) × 100**

Always state what counts as a conversion and whether the base is sessions or users, because the choice changes the result.

## Example

An online store has 40,000 sessions in a month and 800 orders.

**Conversion rate = 800 ÷ 40,000 × 100 = 2%.**

If the average order is $60, revenue per session is 2% × $60 = $1.20.

## Where conversion rate is used

- **Ecommerce:** sessions to orders.
- **SaaS:** trial sign-ups to paying customers.
- **Lead generation sites:** visits to submitted enquiries.
- **Newsletters:** visitors to subscribers.
- **Affiliate sites:** clicks on affiliate links to commissions.

## Why conversion rate matters for valuation

Conversion rate links [traffic](/glossary/traffic/) to revenue. A buyer who understands it can see how much revenue depends on keeping traffic, and where they might improve results. A sharp change in conversion rate before a sale deserves a question: it can come from a genuine improvement, a change in how conversions are tracked, or a change in traffic mix.

Conversion rates vary widely by industry, price, device and traffic source, so compare a business with its own history rather than with general benchmarks.
