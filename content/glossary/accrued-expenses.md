## How it works

Under [accrual basis accounting](/glossary/accrual-basis-accounting/), a cost is recorded in the period it is earned or used, not when the cash leaves the bank. If a business uses a service in December but pays in January, the cost still belongs to December. Until it is paid, it sits on the [balance sheet](/glossary/balance-sheet/) as a liability called an accrued expense.

Common examples for a small online business:

- **Wages and contractor pay** earned but not yet paid out.
- **Interest** on a loan that builds up between payment dates.
- **Services used but not yet invoiced**, such as a month of hosting or ad spend billed afterwards.
- **Taxes** owed on profit already earned.

Accrued expenses differ from [accounts payable](/glossary/accounts-payable/). Payables are bills the business has received. Accruals are costs it knows it owes but has not been billed for yet.

## Example

A store closes its books on June 30. Its freelance editor worked 40 hours in June at $25 an hour and will invoice in July. The store has also used $300 of ad spend that the platform bills on July 5.

- Editor: 40 × $25 = $1,000
- Ad spend: $300
- **Accrued expenses at June 30 = $1,000 + $300 = $1,300**

If the owner ignored these, June profit would look $1,300 higher than it really was.

## Why it matters in a valuation

Valuation usually starts from profit, and profit is only accurate if costs are recorded in the right period. A seller who leaves out accruals makes the business look more profitable. Buyers often check:

- Whether the profit figure includes all costs the period used, not only the ones paid.
- How large the accruals are compared with cash, since they reduce [working capital](/glossary/working-capital/) and the [current ratio](/glossary/current-ratio/).
- Whether a one-off catch-up of unpaid costs is hiding in the latest months.

Adjusting profit for missing accruals can lower the number a multiple is applied to, which changes the price. VALUERAQ's [methodology](/methodology/) explains how profit is adjusted before a default range is shown.
