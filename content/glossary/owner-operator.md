## How it works

This page is about owner-operated websites, stores, apps, newsletters and software, not trucking, where the same words have a different meaning.

In an owner-operator business, the owner does real work: writing, answering customers, managing ads, handling orders or fixing the product. The business earns money partly because of that work. The profit buyers see is often the owner's pay and the business's profit mixed together.

That is why buyers use [seller's discretionary earnings (SDE)](/glossary/sde/): profit before the owner's pay. It shows what a new owner-operator could earn. See [how to calculate SDE](/guides/how-to-calculate-sde/).

## Example

Two hypothetical websites each have SDE of $60,000 a year.

| Site | Owner hours per week | What a buyer sees |
|---|---|---|
| A | 8 | A mostly hands-off business a buyer could run part time |
| B | 45 | A full-time job a buyer must take on, or replace by hiring |

VALUERAQ's default adjustments, from the [methodology](/methodology/), raise a range by 5% for under 10 owner hours a week and lower it by 10% for over 40. Using the website default range of 2.5x to 4.0x:

- **Site A:** multiples of 2.63x to 4.2x give $157,500 to $252,000.
- **Site B:** multiples of 2.25x to 3.6x give $135,000 to $216,000.

The same profit, but the midpoints differ: Site A is $60,000 × 3.36 = $201,600 and Site B is $60,000 × 2.88 = $172,800, a gap of $28,800. (Other ends: $60,000 × 2.625 = $157,500; × 4.2 = $252,000; × 2.25 = $135,000; × 3.6 = $216,000.) These are default adjustments for illustration, not market data.

## Why it matters in a valuation

Buyers want to know what happens after you leave. Ask yourself:

- Who could do your job tomorrow, and what would they need?
- Are the processes written down?
- Do customers or members come for you personally?
- Would you stay for a handover period?

The risk is covered in [key person risk](/glossary/key-person-risk/). To reduce it before selling, document the routines, train a person or contractor to do the core tasks, and offer a [transition period](/glossary/transition-period/) where you stay on to hand over. More ideas are in [increase website value before selling](/guides/increase-website-value-before-selling/). Cutting your weekly hours over the year before a sale can both raise the multiple and show a buyer the business runs without you.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is an owner-operator business hard to sell?

Not necessarily, but it is usually priced lower than a hands-off one with the same profit, because the buyer takes on the work. Clear documentation and a handover plan help.

### Is an owner-operator the same as a sole proprietor?

No. Owner-operator describes how involved the owner is. Sole proprietor describes the legal structure. An owner-operator business can be a company, and a sole proprietor can hire a team.
