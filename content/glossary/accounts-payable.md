## How it works

When an online store orders stock on credit, or a website owner receives a freelancer's invoice due in 30 days, the business has received something but not yet paid. The unpaid amount is accounts payable. It sits on the [balance sheet](/glossary/balance-sheet/) as a short-term liability and falls as the bills are paid.

It is the mirror image of [accounts receivable](/glossary/accounts-receivable/), which is money customers owe the business.

Accounts payable covers ordinary bills for goods and services. It does not usually include long-term loans, which are listed separately.

**Accounts payable = total of unpaid supplier invoices on a given date**

## Example

An ecommerce store looks at its books on the last day of the month. The figures are made up.

| Unpaid invoice | Amount | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Stock from supplier | $12,000 | in 30 days |
| Freelance designer | $1,500 | in 14 days |
| Shipping carrier | $2,500 | in 20 days |
| **Accounts payable** | **$16,000** | |

Check: 12,000 + 1,500 + 2,500 = 16,000.

If the store holds $22,000 in cash and is owed $9,000 by customers, its short-term position is 22,000 + 9,000 − 16,000 = $15,000 before other bills. That calculation is a rough form of [working capital](/glossary/working-capital/).

## Why accounts payable matters in a valuation

- **It shows how the business pays for growth.** If a seller leans on late payments to suppliers, cash looks healthier than it is. A buyer who takes over may have to catch up.
- **It affects what changes hands.** In many sales the seller pays bills incurred before closing, or the price is adjusted for them. Make sure the contract says who pays which invoices.
- **It tests the quality of the books.** Unpaid invoices that do not appear in the accounts, or very old ones, are a [due diligence](/glossary/due-diligence/) warning sign.
- **It affects cash timing.** Longer terms with suppliers can ease [cash flow](/glossary/cash-flow/), but suppliers may change terms after a sale, so ask whether any terms depend on the owner's relationship.

Ask for a list of unpaid supplier invoices with dates, plus a note of any disputes, as part of the request list for the [data room](/glossary/data-room/).
