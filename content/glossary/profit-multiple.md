## Formula

**Profit multiple = price ÷ annual profit**

Website marketplaces often quote a **monthly multiple** instead: price ÷ monthly profit. Divide a monthly multiple by 12 to get the annual one. A "36× multiple" on a website listing means 36 months of profit, or 3× annual profit.

## Example

A newsletter earns $5,000 a month in net profit, or $60,000 a year, and sells for $210,000.

- Annual profit multiple: $210,000 ÷ $60,000 = **3.5×**
- Monthly multiple: $210,000 ÷ $5,000 = **42 months**

## Monthly to annual multiples

Listings quote either form. Divide months by 12 to get years:

| Monthly multiple | Annual multiple |
|---|---|
| 24 months | 2.0× |
| 30 months | 2.5× |
| 36 months | 3.0× |
| 42 months | 3.5× |
| 48 months | 4.0× |

## Using a profit multiple to price a business

Work backwards from the profit you can prove. A site with $48,000 in annual profit, at 2.5× to 4.0×, gives a range of $48,000 × 2.5 = $120,000 to $48,000 × 4.0 = $192,000. At the 3.2× midpoint the figure is $153,600.

## What moves a profit multiple

| Higher multiple | Lower multiple |
|---|---|
| Several years of stable or growing profit | Short history or declining profit |
| Diverse traffic, customers and revenue sources | Dependence on one channel, customer or platform |
| Few owner hours, documented processes | Heavy owner involvement |
| Verified, clean records | Unverifiable or mixed personal and business finances |

## Profit vs revenue multiples

Profit multiples reflect what the owner keeps, which is why they're standard for owner-run online businesses. [Revenue multiples](/glossary/revenue-multiple/) are more common for SaaS. See [revenue vs profit multiples](/guides/revenue-vs-profit-multiples/).

The profit in the formula is usually [SDE](/glossary/sde/). To build it step by step from a profit and loss statement, see [how to calculate SDE](/guides/how-to-calculate-sde/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a higher profit multiple better?

For a seller, yes: it means a higher price for the same profit. For a buyer, a higher multiple means a longer payback, so it should be backed by growth or low risk.

### Does the profit multiple use revenue or profit?

Profit. Revenue is only a starting point; costs must come out first. The same price divided by revenue gives a [revenue multiple](/glossary/revenue-multiple/), a different number.

### Can I convert a profit multiple into a payback period?

Roughly, yes. A 3× multiple means the buyer pays about three years of current profit, so payback is about three years if profit stays flat. It is a simplification that ignores growth and taxes.
