## How it works

When someone types a question into a search engine and clicks one of the ordinary results (not an ad), the visit counts as organic search traffic in most analytics tools. It costs nothing per click, but it is not free: it comes from content, site quality and links that someone paid for in time or money.

Analytics tools group visits by channel. Organic search sits beside paid search, direct, referral, social and email. The grouping depends on the tool's rules, so the same site can show slightly different organic numbers in two tools. Use one tool and keep the same settings over time.

**Organic share = organic visits ÷ total visits**

## Example

A blog had 60,000 visits last month.

| Channel | Visits | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Organic search | 48,000 | 80% |
| Direct | 6,000 | 10% |
| Email | 3,600 | 6% |
| Social | 2,400 | 4% |

Organic share: 48,000 ÷ 60,000 = **80%**. The site has plenty of visitors, but four of every five arrive through search.

## Why it matters in a valuation

Organic traffic is cheap and repeats, so it supports earnings. It is also outside the owner's control. A change in how a search engine ranks pages can move a site's visits quickly, and a buyer treats a high organic share as risk.

VALUERAQ's [Website Valuation Tool](/tools/website-valuation/) asks for the share of traffic from the largest channel, often organic search. Its default rules lower the multiple by 6% when that share is over 60% up to 80%, and by 12% when it is over 80%. Those are published assumptions on the [methodology](/methodology/) page, not market data. In the example above, 80% falls in the 6% band.

Buyers also look at whether organic traffic is steady, whether it is spread across many pages or a few, and whether it leads to sales; see [conversion rate](/glossary/conversion-rate/). They will want to confirm the numbers, as described in [how to verify website traffic](/guides/how-to-verify-website-traffic/).

## Related terms

[Traffic](/glossary/traffic/) covers all channels. A backlink is a link from another site, one of the things that can help pages earn organic visits. [Conversion rate](/glossary/conversion-rate/) shows how many visits turn into sales or sign-ups.
