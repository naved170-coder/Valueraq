## Formula

**Revenue multiple = price ÷ annual revenue**

For SaaS, the revenue figure is usually [ARR](/glossary/arr/), and the result is called an ARR multiple.

## Example

A SaaS business with $240,000 ARR sells for $840,000.

**Revenue multiple = $840,000 ÷ $240,000 = 3.5× ARR.**

## When revenue multiples are used

- **SaaS and other subscription businesses**, where recurring revenue is predictable.
- **Growing businesses reinvesting their profit**, where current profit understates value.
- **Quick comparisons** between businesses with similar margins.

## Why margin matters

A revenue multiple ignores costs. Two businesses with the same revenue can have very different profits, so the same revenue multiple can mean very different profit multiples:

**Profit multiple = revenue multiple ÷ profit margin.**

At 3.5× revenue, a business with a 40% margin is priced at 8.75× profit; one with an 80% margin at about 4.4× profit. That's why revenue multiples should always be read alongside margin. See [revenue vs profit multiples](/guides/revenue-vs-profit-multiples/).
