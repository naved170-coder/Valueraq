## Two meanings on {{BRAND}}

The word "marketplace" comes up in two ways in digital business valuation:

1. **A marketplace business**, which you might buy or sell: a platform that connects two groups, such as freelancers and clients, or makers and shoppers.
2. **A marketplace channel** that another business sells through, such as a large retail marketplace or an app store. Heavy reliance on one channel is a risk factor.

{{BRAND}} itself operates a marketplace for [digital businesses for sale](/businesses-for-sale/).

## Metrics for marketplace businesses

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Gross merchandise value (GMV) | Total value of transactions on the platform |
| Take rate | Platform revenue ÷ GMV |
| Liquidity | How often listings find a buyer, and how quickly |
| Repeat usage | Share of buyers and sellers who return |

## Example

A marketplace for handmade furniture processes $500,000 of sales a year and charges a 10% commission. Its revenue is $50,000, and its take rate is 10%. Buyers value it on the profit that revenue produces, and on whether both sides keep coming back.

## Selling through a marketplace channel

For a store or app that sells through someone else's marketplace, the platform sets fees, rules and visibility. Account suspensions or fee changes can hit revenue fast, and some seller accounts can't be transferred in a sale. {{BRAND}}'s [ecommerce](/tools/ecommerce-valuation/) and [app](/tools/app-valuation/) tools adjust the multiple when one channel provides most sales.
