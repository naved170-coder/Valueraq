## Summary

| Data | Source | How it's labelled |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue, profit, MRR, growth, churn and other inputs | Entered by the user | User-provided |
| Default base multiples and adjustment sizes | {{BRAND}} editorial assumptions (methodology v1.0) | Platform assumption |
| Annual profit, ARR, adjusted multiples, value ranges | Calculated from the two above | Calculated |
| Listing figures | Provided by sellers | Unverified, unless a verification status is shown |

## User-provided data

The tools don't connect to your analytics, bank or payment accounts. Every figure comes from what you type in. We don't store it unless you save a report to your account, and saved reports are private.

## Platform assumptions

The default multiple ranges and the size of each adjustment are editorial judgements by the {{BRAND}} team, informed by how small online businesses are commonly discussed and priced. **They are not derived from a measured dataset of completed sales.** We say so wherever they appear, and we publish them in full in the [methodology](/methodology/) so you can apply your own judgement.

## What we don't have yet

We don't currently license a third-party dataset of digital business transactions, and we don't publish market-wide averages, benchmarks or trend statistics. When a page on {{BRAND}} states a formula result (for example, how monthly churn compounds over a year), that is arithmetic, not a market statistic.

## Marketplace data

Asking prices, revenue, profit, traffic and other listing facts are provided by sellers. Implied multiples on listing pages are calculated from those figures. A listing shows a [verification status](/verification/) only when our team has checked specific evidence.

## Future aggregated data

As the marketplace grows, we may publish aggregated, anonymised statistics from listings and valuations, for example ranges of asking multiples by category. Before publishing any dataset we will state its period, sample size, method and limitations, and we will never publish information that identifies a seller or a private report. Until then, no such statistics appear on the site.

## Corrections

If you believe a figure or statement on {{BRAND}} is wrong, tell us through the [contact page](/contact/). Corrections are handled under our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).
