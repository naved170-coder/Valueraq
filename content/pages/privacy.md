## Data we collect

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Email address, name (optional), password (stored as a secure hash) | When you create an account | To provide your account |
| Figures you enter into a tool | When you calculate a valuation | To calculate the result. Stored only if you save a report |
| Saved reports | When you save one | To show them to you later |
| Listing details and private website URL | When you list a business | To review and publish your listing; the URL is shown only to reviewers |
| Inquiry messages | When you contact a seller | To deliver them to the seller |
| Contact form messages | When you write to us | To reply |
| Billing information | When you subscribe or pay | Processed by our payment provider; we don't store card numbers |
| Usage and performance data | When you use the site | First-party analytics described below |

## Cookies and analytics

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in and protect forms. Our own analytics sets three first-party cookies that expire after 30 minutes of inactivity: a random visit identifier, the channel you arrived from (for example search or direct), and the first page you visited. We also measure page speed. This data isn't shared with advertising networks, and we don't use advertising scripts.

We also use **Google Analytics** to understand how the site is used: which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are, and what kind of device they use. Until you choose **Accept** on the cookie notice, Google Analytics runs without cookies and receives only anonymous, aggregated measurements. If you accept, it sets cookies (their names start with `_ga`) that let it recognise a returning browser for up to two years. We never allow it to use data for advertising. You can change your choice at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of any page. Google Analytics isn't loaded on account or admin pages.

## Who processes data for us

- **Hosting provider:** stores the website and its database.
- **Google (Google Analytics):** receives the usage measurements described above.
- **Payment provider:** processes subscription and featured-listing payments.
- **Email provider**, if we email you.

We don't sell personal data.

## What other users see

Published listings are public, except your website URL, email address and contact details. When you send an inquiry, the seller sees your message and email address. Saved reports are private to you.

## Retention

We keep account data while your account is open. You can delete saved reports at any time. When you close your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data unless we need to keep it to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

## Your choices and rights

You can access and update your account details, delete saved reports, and ask us to delete your account or export your data by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local privacy law.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the [contact page](/contact/).
