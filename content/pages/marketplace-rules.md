## Who can list

You must own the business or have the owner's written authority to sell it, and be able to transfer the assets described in the listing.

## What you can list

Websites, SaaS, AI businesses, apps, newsletters and ecommerce businesses that operate lawfully and earn revenue.

## What's not allowed

- Businesses that break the law or the terms of the platforms they depend on.
- Adult content, gambling without the required licences, weapons, or illegal products or services.
- Businesses built mainly on copied content, fake reviews, spam or manipulated traffic.
- "Business in a box" offers, templates or courses presented as operating businesses.
- Listings for businesses you don't own.

## Accuracy standards

- Revenue and profit must be averages for the last 12 months (or the business's full life if shorter), not a best month.
- Profit must be after all real business costs, before the owner's own pay.
- Material facts that affect value, such as a recent traffic drop, a lost major customer or a platform warning, must be disclosed.
- Descriptions must be plain text written by you about the business. Links, contact details and keyword stuffing are removed.

## Moderation

Every new or edited listing is reviewed before it's published. Reviewers check completeness, consistency and the rules above, and may ask for evidence. Listings that are incomplete or too short can go live but won't be shown to search engines until they meet our quality standard. Duplicate listings are rejected.

## Removal

We may remove or suspend a listing that breaks these rules, receives credible reports of false information, or is abandoned. When a listing is withdrawn or sold, its page is updated accordingly.

## Fees

Listing is free. Optional featured placement is available for live listings; see [pricing](/pricing/). {{BRAND}} doesn't take a commission and doesn't handle payment between buyers and sellers.
