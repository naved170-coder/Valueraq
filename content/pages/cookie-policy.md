## What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser so it can remember something between pages or visits, such as the fact that you are logged in.

## Cookies we set

| Name | Type | What it does | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `session` | Essential | Keeps you logged in and protects forms against misuse. | Up to 30 days, or until you log out |
| `vq_sid` | Our own statistics | A random number that groups the pages of one visit together. It isn't linked to your name or account. | 30 minutes after your last page |
| `vq_ch` | Our own statistics | How you arrived, for example from a search engine or by typing the address. | 30 minutes after your last page |
| `vq_lp` | Our own statistics | The first page you opened in this visit. | 30 minutes after your last page |
| `_ga` and `_ga_…` | Google Analytics | Lets Google Analytics recognise a returning browser so visits are counted correctly. **Set only if you choose Accept.** | Up to 2 years |

We also store your answer to the cookie notice (accept or refuse) in your browser's local storage under the name `vq_cookie_choice`, so we don't ask again on every page. It isn't sent to us.

## Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see which pages are used and how people find the site. Until you choose **Accept**, it runs without cookies and Google receives only anonymous, combined measurements. If you accept, the `_ga` cookies above are set. We have switched off every advertising use of this data. Google Analytics isn't loaded on account or admin pages.

## Cookies we don't use

We don't use advertising cookies, we don't sell data collected through cookies, and we don't let advertising networks track you on {{BRAND}}.

## Your choices

- **Change your answer:** use the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of any page to accept or refuse Google Analytics cookies at any time.
- **Remove cookies:** you can delete cookies in your browser's settings. Deleting the `session` cookie logs you out.
- **Block cookies:** you can set your browser to block cookies. The valuation tools still work, but you won't be able to log in.
