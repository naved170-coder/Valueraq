## Who writes our content

Guides and glossary entries are written and maintained by the {{BRAND}} Editorial Team. Where a named author writes a piece, their name and relevant experience appear on it, and we only state qualifications they actually hold.

## Standards

Every guide aims to be:

- **Accurate:** factual claims are checked; formulas and examples are calculated, not estimated.
- **Specific:** explaining what to do and why, with worked examples.
- **Consistent:** definitions and the methodology match across every page.
- **Honest about uncertainty:** each guide states the limits of what it covers.

## Statistics and sources

We don't publish market statistics, benchmarks, search volumes or averages we can't source. When a page relies on external information, we name and link the source and the date. Figures produced by {{BRAND}}'s own tools are labelled as calculated, and default assumptions are labelled as assumptions. See [data sources](/data-sources/).

## Use of AI

AI tools may help with research, outlines or first drafts. A person reviews every AI-assisted piece for accuracy, originality and usefulness before publication, and we record that review internally. We don't publish pages generated automatically in bulk.



## Independence

Guides are not sponsored, and we don't accept payment to mention or recommend products. Any paid features, now or in future, don't affect our guides or listing reviews. If we ever use affiliate links, they will be disclosed on the page where they appear.

## Updates

Important pages show when they were last updated or reviewed. We update the date only when we make a substantive change, not to make a page look newer. The methodology has its own version history.

## Corrections

If you spot an error, tell us through the [contact page](/contact/). We'll review it, correct the page if needed, and note significant corrections on the page.
