## Verification statuses

| Status | What we checked |
|---|---|
| Unverified | The listing passed moderation. Figures are as stated by the seller. |
| Revenue verified | Stated revenue matches read-only access to, or original statements from, the revenue source (payment processor, billing system, affiliate or ad-network dashboard) for at least the last 12 months. |
| Traffic verified | Stated traffic matches read-only analytics access for at least the last 12 months. |
| Fully verified | Revenue and traffic are verified, and the seller has shown control of the domain or main accounts. |

## How verification works

1. The seller requests verification and grants read-only access or provides original statements.
2. A reviewer compares the evidence with the listing's figures.
3. If they match within reasonable rounding, the status is applied. If not, the seller is asked to correct the listing.
4. The status applies to the period checked. If the seller later edits their figures, the listing is reviewed again.

## What verification doesn't cover

Verification confirms that specific figures match specific evidence at the time of checking. It doesn't confirm future performance, profit (unless stated), code quality, legal compliance, ownership of every asset, or the absence of undisclosed problems. It doesn't replace a buyer's own [due diligence](/guides/digital-business-due-diligence/).
