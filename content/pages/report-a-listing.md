## What you can report

- A listing with false or misleading figures, or one that hides a material fact.
- A listing for a business the seller doesn't own or has no right to sell.
- A suspected scam, for example a seller asking for direct payment or for your passwords.
- A duplicate listing, spam, or a business that isn't allowed under the [marketplace rules](/marketplace-rules/).
- Abusive or threatening messages.
- Text, images or other material on {{BRAND}} that copies your work without permission.

## How to report a listing

Email [hello@valueraq.com](mailto:hello@valueraq.com) or use the [contact form](/contact/#form) and choose **A listing** or **Report a problem**. Include:

1. the **listing number** (shown under the title of every listing) or the page address;
2. what you believe is wrong, in a sentence or two;
3. any evidence you have, such as a screenshot or a link;
4. how we can reach you if we have questions.

## What happens next

1. We read every report, usually within two business days.
2. We compare the listing with our rules and may ask the seller for evidence.
3. If the report is upheld, we ask for a correction, suspend the listing or remove it, and may close the seller's account.
4. We tell you the outcome where we can. We don't share your identity with the seller without your agreement, unless the law requires it.

We can't settle disputes about a sale between a buyer and a seller, recover money, or give legal advice. If you have lost money, contact your bank, the escrow service and the police or fraud-reporting service in your country straight away. See [buyer safety](/buyer-safety/).

## Copyright complaints

If you believe material on {{BRAND}} infringes your copyright, email [hello@valueraq.com](mailto:hello@valueraq.com) with the subject "Copyright complaint" and include:

1. your name and contact details, and who you act for if you aren't the owner;
2. a description of the work you say has been copied, with a link to the original if there is one;
3. the address of the page on {{BRAND}} where the material appears;
4. a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorised by the owner or by law;
5. a statement that the information you have given is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner;
6. your signature, which can be your typed full name.

We remove or disable material when a complaint appears valid and tell the person who posted it. If they believe the material was removed by mistake, they can reply with their reasons and we will review the decision. Accounts that repeatedly post infringing material are closed.

Knowingly making a false report or a false copyright complaint may have legal consequences.
