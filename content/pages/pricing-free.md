## Is there any charge at all?

No. During our launch period there are no plans to buy, no listing fees and no commission. You don't need a payment card to create an account or to use any feature.

## Will it stay free?

The valuation tools, calculators, methodology, guides and glossary, and browsing the marketplace will always be free. After the launch period we may introduce optional paid plans for extra features. If we do, the prices will be published on this page before anything changes, and reports you've saved and businesses you've listed will stay in your account.

## What we don't do

{{BRAND}} doesn't handle payment between buyers and sellers and doesn't broker deals. Buyers and sellers agree terms directly and should use an escrow service and their own advisers.
