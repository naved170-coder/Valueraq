## Valuing a business

1. **Choose a tool.** There is one each for websites, SaaS, AI businesses, apps, newsletters and ecommerce stores, on the [valuation tools](/tools/) page.
2. **Enter your figures.** Revenue, profit or recurring revenue, age, growth and a few questions about risk. No account is needed and nothing you enter is published.
3. **See the range.** You get a low, middle and high estimate, the multiple used and every adjustment with its reason. The method is published in full in the [methodology](/methodology/).
4. **Save a report (optional).** Create a free account to save detailed reports. Free accounts keep three; [Pro](/pricing/) removes the limit and adds printable reports.

## Buying a business

1. **Browse.** Open [businesses for sale](/businesses-for-sale/) and pick a category. Filter by price, profit, age and business model.
2. **Read the listing.** Each one shows the seller's figures, the multiples the asking price implies, any verification badge and a set of automatic checks.
3. **Save and get alerts.** With a free account you can save a listing to your watchlist and save a search; we email you if a price drops, a listing sells, or a new listing matches.
4. **Message the seller.** Conversations are private and stay inside your account. You can attach files such as a proof of funds.
5. **Check everything.** Follow the [buyer safety](/buyer-safety/) steps and the [due diligence checklist](/guides/digital-business-due-diligence/).
6. **Agree and pay.** You and the seller agree terms directly and should pay through an independent escrow service. {{BRAND}} isn't part of the sale and takes no commission.

## Selling a business

1. **Value it first.** Run the matching tool so your asking price is one you can explain.
2. **Create a listing.** Listing is free. Fill in the [listing form](/sell/) with your figures and a description; the website address stays private.
3. **Review.** We check every listing against the [marketplace rules](/marketplace-rules/) before it goes live, usually within two business days.
4. **Build trust.** Prove you own the website to get the free ownership badge, and ask for a revenue or traffic [verification](/verification/) badge. Your account shows a listing strength score with tips.
5. **Talk to buyers.** Messages arrive in your account and by email. Your analytics page shows views, saves and messages.
6. **Close the sale.** Agree terms with the buyer, use escrow, hand over the assets, then mark the listing as sold.
