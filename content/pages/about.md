## What we do

{{BRAND}} helps people understand what digital businesses are worth and find ones to buy or sell. The platform has three parts:

- **[Valuation tools](/tools/) and [calculators](/calculators/)** that estimate a business's value from figures the user enters, using a [methodology](/methodology/) we publish in full.
- **A [marketplace](/businesses-for-sale/)** where owners list websites, SaaS, AI businesses, apps, newsletters and ecommerce stores for sale. Every listing is reviewed before publication.
- **[Guides](/guides/) and a [glossary](/glossary/)** explaining how digital businesses are valued, bought and sold.

## How our valuations work

Every tool follows the same approach: a base metric (annual profit or annual recurring revenue), a default multiple range for the business type, and published adjustments for age, growth and risk. Every figure in a result is labelled as user-provided, a platform assumption or calculated. We explain our data sources, and what we don't yet have, on the [data sources](/data-sources/) page.

Our estimates are starting points for decisions, not appraisals, and they aren't investment, tax or legal advice.

## What we don't do

- We don't act as a broker, hold funds or run escrow. Buyers and sellers agree terms directly.
- We don't publish sample or invented listings, reviews, ratings or statistics.
- We don't sell personal data, and private valuation reports are never shown publicly.

## How we make money

{{BRAND}} is free to use while we're in launch: valuations, saved reports, browsing and listing all cost nothing. We don't take a commission on sales. After the launch period we may offer optional paid plans for extra features; any prices will be published on the [pricing page](/pricing/) first, and payment will never affect our guides or how a listing is reviewed.
