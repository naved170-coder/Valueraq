## What is free

| For everyone | For buyers | For sellers |
|---|---|---|
| All six valuation tools and three calculators | Browsing every listing | Listing a business for sale |
| The full methodology and every adjustment | Messaging sellers, with file attachments | Messages from buyers |
| Detailed reports, three saved at a time | Watchlist with price-drop and sold alerts | Views, saves and messages statistics |
| Guides and glossary | Saved searches with email alerts | Website ownership badge and verification requests |

## What is paid

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pro | $29 | $250 | Unlimited saved reports and printable reports. Includes a 14-day free trial with no card. |
| Featured listing | $39 per listing | $350 per listing | Your live listing shown first in its category and marked "Featured". |

Both are optional and can be cancelled at any time. Full details are on the [pricing page](/pricing/), and refunds are covered by the [refund and cancellation policy](/refund-policy/). Online payment isn't open yet; until it is, nothing on {{BRAND}} can be charged for.

## What we never charge

- **No commission or success fee** when a business sells.
- **No listing fee** for a standard listing.
- **No fee to buyers** for contacting sellers.
- **No fee for verification** or the ownership badge.

Paying for Pro or a Featured listing doesn't change how a listing is reviewed, its verification status or how our guides are written.

## Costs outside {{BRAND}}

A sale usually involves costs paid to other people, which are not {{BRAND}} fees: an escrow service's fee, legal and accounting advice, payment or bank transfer charges, and any taxes due on the sale. Agree with the other side who pays each of these before you sign.

## Changes to prices

If a price changes, the new price is shown on the pricing page before you pay and applies from your next renewal, not to a period you have already paid for.
