## Summary

| | Cancel | Refund |
|---|---|---|
| Free plan and free trial | Nothing to cancel. The 14-day Pro trial needs no card and ends by itself. | Nothing is charged. |
| Pro (monthly or yearly) | Any time, from **Plan and billing** in your account. Pro stays active to the end of the paid period. | Full refund of your **first** payment if you ask within 7 days. A yearly renewal can also be refunded within 7 days of the renewal date. |
| Featured listing (monthly or yearly) | Any time, from **Plan and billing**. The listing stays featured to the end of the paid period. | Not refundable once the listing has been featured, except in the cases listed below. |

Free features, including the valuation tools, standard listings and messaging, never involve a payment, so this policy only concerns Pro and Featured listing subscriptions. Online payment is not open yet; this policy applies from the day it opens.

## Cancelling

1. Log in and open **Plan and billing** in your account.
2. Choose **Manage or cancel subscriptions and invoices** and cancel the subscription.
3. You keep Pro, or the listing stays featured, until the end of the period you have already paid for. No further payments are taken.

Cancelling stops future payments. It does not by itself refund the current period; see below for when a refund applies. Deleting or withdrawing a listing, or marking it sold, does not cancel its Featured subscription automatically, so cancel that subscription as well.

## Pro refunds

- **First payment:** if Pro isn't right for you, ask within 7 days of your first payment, monthly or yearly, and we refund it in full. Pro then ends straight away.
- **Yearly renewals:** if a yearly plan renews and you didn't mean it to, ask within 7 days of the renewal date for a full refund of that renewal.
- **Monthly renewals:** not refundable. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next payment.
- **Part-used periods:** we don't give partial refunds for the unused part of a month or year after the 7 days.

## Featured listing refunds

Featuring starts as soon as payment is confirmed, so the fee isn't refundable once the listing has been shown as featured. That includes when the business sells, when you withdraw the listing, and when we remove the listing for breaking the [marketplace rules](/marketplace-rules/).

We do refund a Featured listing payment when:

- the listing was never shown as featured because of a fault on our side;
- we remove or unpublish the listing for a reason other than a breach of the marketplace rules; in that case we refund the unused part of the period.

## Refunds we always make

- A payment taken twice, or for the wrong amount.
- A payment taken after you cancelled.
- A payment for a feature that didn't work because of a fault on our side that we couldn't fix within a reasonable time.

## How to ask for a refund

Email [help@valueraq.com](mailto:help@valueraq.com) from the email address on your account, or use the [contact form](/contact/#form) and choose **Billing**. Tell us which payment it is and why you're asking. We reply within two business days.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank or card provider usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show it. We can't refund to a different card or account.

## What isn't covered

{{BRAND}} isn't a party to any sale between a buyer and a seller and doesn't hold or handle the purchase price. We can't refund money paid to a seller, an escrow service, a broker or anyone else. A valuation estimate that differs from the price a business later sells for isn't grounds for a refund; estimates are explained in our [methodology](/methodology/).

## Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the consumer law of the country you live in. Where that law gives you a longer cancellation period or a right to a refund, it applies.

## Changes

If we change this policy, the new version applies to payments made after the date shown at the top of the page.
