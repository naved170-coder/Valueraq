## Valuation estimates

The valuation tools, calculators and reports give an estimated range based on the figures you enter and the assumptions set out in our [methodology](/methodology/). The default multiples are {{BRAND}}'s own published assumptions, as explained on the [data sources](/data-sources/) page; they are not taken from a measured set of completed sales.

An estimate is not an appraisal, an audit, a fairness opinion or an offer to buy. The price a business actually sells for depends on the buyer, the terms, the evidence behind the figures and the market at the time, and it can be well above or below the estimated range. If the figures entered are wrong, the estimate will be wrong.

## Not professional advice

Nothing on {{BRAND}}, including the guides, glossary, reports and emails, is investment, financial, accounting, tax or legal advice, and nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any business. We don't know your circumstances. Speak to a qualified adviser before making a decision.

## Marketplace listings

Listings are written by sellers. We review each one against our [marketplace rules](/marketplace-rules/) before it goes live, but unless a listing shows a [verification status](/verification/), we haven't checked its figures against evidence. A verification status covers only what the verification page says it covers, for the period checked.

The **automatic checks** shown on listings are worked out from the seller's own figures and our published assumptions. They are prompts for a buyer's questions. They don't confirm that a listing is accurate, safe or fairly priced.

{{BRAND}} isn't a broker, agent, escrow service or party to any sale. Buyers and sellers deal with each other directly, at their own risk, and buyers must carry out their own due diligence.
