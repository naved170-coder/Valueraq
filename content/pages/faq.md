## Frequently asked questions

### What is a website valuation calculator?

A website valuation calculator estimates what a website could sell for from its financial figures. {{BRAND}}'s [Website Valuation Tool](/tools/website-valuation/) multiplies annual net profit by a multiple adjusted for age, growth, traffic sources, monetization and owner workload, and shows every step.

### How is a website valued?

Most small websites are valued as a multiple of their annual net profit, with the multiple adjusted for how durable that profit is. See [how to value a website](/guides/website-valuation/).

### What factors affect a digital business's value?

Profit or recurring revenue, age, growth trend, dependence on one channel or customer, the owner's workload, and, for SaaS and AI, churn and gross margin. The exact effect of each on {{BRAND}}'s estimates is in the [methodology](/methodology/#adjustments).

### How accurate is an online business valuation?

It depends on the accuracy of the figures entered and on how typical the business is. {{BRAND}}'s estimates use published default assumptions, not a transaction dataset, so treat them as a reasoned starting point. Actual prices depend on negotiation, deal terms and buyers.

### What is a SaaS valuation multiple?

A SaaS valuation multiple is the number by which a SaaS business's ARR (or profit) is multiplied to estimate its value. See [revenue multiple](/glossary/revenue-multiple/) and the [SaaS valuation guide](/guides/saas-valuation/).

### How do I value a SaaS business?

Calculate ARR (MRR × 12), start from a base multiple, and adjust it for growth, churn, gross margin, customer concentration and owner involvement. The [SaaS Valuation Tool](/tools/saas-valuation/) does this for you.

### Is VALUERAQ free?

Yes. All tools, calculators, guides and marketplace listings are free. An optional Pro plan adds unlimited saved reports and extra report features. See [pricing](/pricing/).

### Do you store the figures I enter?

Only if you save a report to your account. Saved reports are private to you, not visible to other users or search engines, and you can delete them at any time.

### Can I sell my website through the marketplace?

Yes. Create a free account, submit a listing with your figures and a description, and our team reviews it before it goes live. See the [marketplace rules](/marketplace-rules/).

### Does VALUERAQ verify listings?

Listing figures come from sellers. Some listings carry a [verification status](/verification/) showing that our team checked specific evidence. Buyers should always complete their own [due diligence](/guides/digital-business-due-diligence/).

### Does VALUERAQ take a commission on sales?

No. We don't act as a broker or hold funds. Buyers and sellers agree terms directly and should use escrow for payment.

### Are the valuations financial advice?

No. They are estimates for information, not appraisals, and not investment, tax or legal advice.
