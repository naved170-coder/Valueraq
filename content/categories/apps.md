## Buying an app

Buying an app usually means acquiring the source code, the app store listings (transferred to your developer account), backend services, the user base, in-app purchase or subscription products, and any brand assets. Revenue from subscriptions and in-app purchases runs through the app stores, which take a commission and set rules the app must follow.

| Revenue model | What to check |
|---|---|
| Subscriptions | Renewal rates, trial conversion, refunds |
| In-app purchases | Spend per paying user, reliance on a few heavy spenders |
| Advertising | Ad revenue per user, ad-network dependence, user volume trend |
| Paid download | New sales trend, store ranking and visibility |

## Metrics to compare on every listing

- **Monthly net profit** after store commissions, servers and paid acquisition.
- **Downloads and active users** over time, and retention by install cohort.
- **Platforms**: iOS, Android or both.
- **Where users come from**: store search, featuring, paid ads, referrals.
- **Implied profit multiple** shown on each listing.

## How apps are valued

Profitable small apps are generally valued as a multiple of annual profit. {{BRAND}}'s default assumption is 2× to 3.5× annual profit before adjustments for age, growth, revenue model, platforms, channel concentration and workload. See the [methodology](/methodology/) or check a listing with the [App Valuation Tool](/tools/app-valuation/).

## Buying tips specific to apps

1. **Check store console data directly**: downloads, revenue, ratings, crash rates and any policy warnings.
2. **Review the codebase and build process**, including third-party SDKs, whether they are up to date, and whether the app builds on current OS versions.
3. **Confirm the app transfer process** for each store and what it moves (reviews, subscriptions, in-app products).
4. **Look at acquisition costs** if growth comes from paid ads.
5. **Review privacy disclosures** in each store listing against the data the app actually collects.

## Frequently asked questions

### How are mobile apps valued?

Most small profitable apps are valued on a multiple of annual profit, adjusted for growth, how revenue is earned and how dependent the app is on one channel. Downloads alone don't set the price.

### Do subscriptions transfer to the new owner?

When an app is transferred within the same store, active subscriptions generally move with it, but each store has its own rules and conditions. Check current store documentation before agreeing terms.
