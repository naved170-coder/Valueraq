## Short answer

A store transfer has two parts: moving the store account and the domain, and rebuilding everything that is tied to the seller as a person or business, such as payment accounts, advertising accounts and some apps. Shopify publishes its own steps for moving ownership, and they change, so read the current ones in its help center before you start. This page covers the order of operations around them.

## What moves and what you rebuild

| Item | Usually | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Products, pages, theme and order history | Stay in the store | Take a backup export before the handover |
| Domain name | Moved or re-pointed | Agree who pays any fees; ask the registrar about waiting periods |
| Paid apps | Often tied to the store's billing | Check each app and its monthly cost |
| Payment processor account | Tied to the account holder's identity | The buyer usually opens their own |
| Advertising accounts and pixels | Often tied to a person or business | Rebuild, or share access under written terms |
| Email tools and customer list | Depend on the tool and privacy rules | Ask a lawyer what may be passed on |
| Supplier accounts | Relationships, not files | The seller introduces the buyer |
| Reviews and social profiles | Depend on how each is set up | Check each one before you rely on it |

## Order of operations

1. **Agree what is included** in the sale agreement and the [letter of intent](/glossary/letter-of-intent/).
2. **Fund escrow.** The buyer pays an independent service, which holds the money. See [how escrow works](/guides/how-escrow-works-for-website-sales/).
3. **Back up** the products, customers, orders and theme.
4. **Transfer the store account** following Shopify's current steps.
5. **Move the domain** and test that the site loads.
6. **Rebuild** payments, shipping, advertising and email under the buyer's own logins.
7. **Introduce the suppliers.**
8. **Check the list together,** then release escrow.

## First 30 days

- **Week 1:** Place a test order, confirm payouts reach the buyer and check every email the store sends.
- **Week 2:** Review supplier orders and customer messages.
- **Week 3:** Compare sales with the same weeks last year.
- **Week 4:** Close any open handover tasks and end the seller's support period.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I just copy a store instead of transferring it?

Copying the contents is possible, but you lose order history, reviews and ranking that live on the original. Decide which of those you are paying for.

### Do my customers need to do anything?

Usually not, but tell them if the name on emails, receipts or the returns address changes.

### Who pays the app fees during the handover?

Agree it in writing. Many apps bill monthly, so settle who pays for the month in which the handover happens.

### What if something does not transfer?

Hold back the release of escrow until it is resolved, or agree in writing what the seller will do.
