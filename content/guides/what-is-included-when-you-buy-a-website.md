## The short answer

The only things you can count on are the ones written into the sale agreement. As a starting point, most website sales include:

- **The domain name** and the right to move it to your registrar account.
- **The site itself**: files, database, theme or code, and all content.
- **Hosting and platform accounts**, or a clean move of the site to yours.
- **Business accounts tied to the site**, such as analytics, ad or affiliate accounts, where the program's rules allow a transfer.

## What is often left out

These are worth naming in the contract, one by one:

- Email lists and the right to keep emailing them.
- Social media accounts and the brand name or logo files.
- Supplier, writer or developer contracts.
- Passwords to third-party tools paid for by the seller.
- Training or help after the sale. Agree how long it lasts and what it covers.

The U.S. Small Business Administration advises asking about "contracts, leases, existing cash flow and inventory" when you buy a business, and lists financial statements and tax returns among the documents to evaluate ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business/buy-existing-business-or-franchise)).

## A simple way to check

Write a list of everything you need to run the site on day one without the seller. Tick off each item against the draft agreement. Anything you cannot tick is either missing from the deal or something you must set up yourself. The steps for moving the assets are in [how to transfer a website](/guides/how-to-transfer-a-website-to-a-new-owner/).

Pay through an independent [escrow](/glossary/escrow/) service and release the money only when the handover is complete. VALUERAQ lets buyers message sellers for free and takes no commission, but it is not a party to the sale.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the domain always included?

Usually, but confirm it. Check the registrar account and that the domain is not locked or held in another name.

### Do I get the traffic and rankings?

You get the site that earns them. Rankings can move after a sale, so look at [how to verify website traffic](/guides/how-to-verify-website-traffic/) and ask for historical reports.

### What if something is missing after the sale?

If it was not in the contract, your options are limited. Have a lawyer review the agreement before you sign.
