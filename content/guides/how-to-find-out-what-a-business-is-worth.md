## The short version

1. **Find the yearly profit.** Add up the last 12 months of net profit. Leave your own salary out of the costs. This figure is called [SDE](/glossary/sde/).
2. **Choose a multiple.** The [multiple](/glossary/valuation-multiple/) depends on the type of business and how risky its earnings are.
3. **Multiply.** Yearly profit × multiple = an estimate of value.
4. **Check it.** Compare the answer with the other two approaches below.

## Three ways to look at the same business

The U.S. Small Business Administration describes three common approaches to valuing a business you plan to sell:

| Approach | What it asks | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Market | What do similar businesses sell for? | Most small businesses with steady profit |
| Income | What will it earn in future, allowing for risk? | Businesses with predictable or growing income |
| Assets | What are the assets worth, minus what is owed? | Businesses with little or no profit |

A profit multiple is the everyday form of the market approach. It is quick, and it uses numbers you already have.

## A worked example

An online store keeps $5,000 a month after all costs, before the owner's pay.

- Yearly profit: $5,000 × 12 = $60,000
- {{BRAND}}'s default range for an ecommerce business is 2.0 to 3.5 times yearly profit, before adjustments
- Low: $60,000 × 2.0 = $120,000
- High: $60,000 × 3.5 = $210,000

Stock is normally paid for on top, at what it cost. These multiples are {{BRAND}}'s published assumptions, set out in the [methodology](/methodology/). They are not records of real sales.

## What moves the answer

- **Age.** A longer record gives a buyer more to rely on.
- **Trend.** Rising revenue lifts the multiple and falling revenue cuts it.
- **Your hours.** A business that needs you full time is worth less to someone else.
- **Reliance on one source.** One customer, one supplier or one traffic channel is a risk.

## When to pay for a formal valuation

A calculator is enough to set expectations or to judge an asking price. Pay a qualified appraiser when the figure will be used in court, for tax, for a loan or in a dispute between partners. The SBA also suggests having an attorney review the sale agreement.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I value a business from revenue alone?

Only roughly. Revenue says nothing about costs, so two businesses with the same sales can have very different values. Revenue multiples are mainly used for subscription software. See [revenue and profit multiples](/guides/revenue-vs-profit-multiples/).

### What if the business makes no profit?

A profit multiple gives no answer. Look at the assets instead: stock, equipment, the domain, the customer list and anything else a buyer could use.

### How do I check someone else's asking price?

Divide the asking price by the yearly profit. The [business multiple calculator](/calculators/business-multiple-calculator/) does this and shows where the result sits against the default range.
