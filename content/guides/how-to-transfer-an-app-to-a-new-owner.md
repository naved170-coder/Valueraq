## The short answer

Transfer the app through the store itself. Do not hand over a password. Apple and Google each have a transfer process that moves the app, its listing and its history from one developer account to another. Both accounts need to be in good standing first.

Check Apple's and Google's current pages before you start, because store rules change.

## Apple App Store

Apple says you need to transfer app ownership "when you've sold the app to another developer" ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/transfer-an-app/overview-of-app-transfer/)). The steps are:

1. Check the transfer criteria. The app must have at least one version released to the App Store, and both accounts must have accepted the latest agreements ([Apple criteria](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/transfer-an-app/app-transfer-criteria/)).
2. Back up all app information.
3. The sending account's Account Holder starts the transfer.
4. The receiving account's Account Holder accepts it.

The Bundle ID stays the same, and the app keeps its reviews and ratings. The Apple Pay merchant ID does not transfer with the app, and Game Center groups and matchmaking configuration do not move either. The app cannot be on pre-order or in review during the transfer.

## Google Play

Google says "both Google Play developer accounts need to be registered and active" ([Google](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6230247?hl=en)). Users, statistics, ratings, reviews and subscriptions transfer with the app. Apps that are paid or have in-app products need an active payments profile on the receiving account.

Not everything moves. Promotions, test groups and earnings reports do not transfer, and orders created before the transfer remain in the original account. A buyer who has no developer account must open one first, and Google lists a one-time US$25 registration fee. Agree who pays it.

## What to settle before you start

| Item | Apple | Google Play |
|---|---|---|
| Who starts it | Sending Account Holder | Sending developer account |
| Reviews and ratings | Move with the app | Move with the app |
| Does not move | Apple Pay merchant ID, Game Center groups | Promotions, test groups, earnings reports |

Also agree who keeps pending payouts, how the source code and third-party accounts are handed over, and when payment is released. Use [escrow](/glossary/escrow/) for the payment, and have a lawyer review the agreement.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I just give the buyer my developer account login?

That is not a transfer, and it leaves ownership unclear. Use the store's process so the app moves to the buyer's own account.

### Do reviews and ratings move with the app?

On both stores, yes, according to Apple's and Google's documentation.
