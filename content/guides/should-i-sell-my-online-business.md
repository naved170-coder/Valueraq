## The short answer

Sell when a sale price is worth more to you than keeping the business would be, once you count your time and the risks you carry. Keep it when it pays you well, is stable, and you still want the work. The decision is a comparison, not a feeling, so put numbers on both sides.

## Compare the two options

| Question | If you keep it | If you sell it |
|---|---|---|
| What do I get? | Annual profit, paid slowly | A lump sum, less costs of sale |
| What do I give up? | Your hours, every week | The income and the business |
| What risk do I carry? | A traffic drop, a platform change, burnout | Deal risk, and any payments held back |
| What can I do with it? | Grow it | Invest it or start something new |

## Example

A website earns **$40,000** a year in profit and takes the owner 25 hours a week. VALUERAQ's default range for a website has a midpoint multiple of 3.2 (see the [methodology](/methodology/); this is an assumption, not market data).

**Value at the midpoint = $40,000 × 3.2 = $128,000.**

Keeping the site, the owner earns $40,000 a year. At that rate it takes **3.2 years** of profit ($128,000 ÷ $40,000) to equal the sale price. If the owner believes the profit will hold or grow for much longer than 3.2 years, keeping may be worth more. If they think traffic is at risk, or they no longer want 25 hours a week of work, the lump sum looks better. The same arithmetic works for any business with your own numbers.

## What to weigh besides the price

- **Trend.** Falling profit usually sells for less later than now; rising profit may be worth keeping a little longer. See [when to sell an online business](/guides/when-to-sell-an-online-business/).
- **Your hours.** Dividing yearly profit by hours worked gives your real hourly return.
- **Concentration.** One traffic source or one customer is a risk you carry every day.
- **What you would do next.** A sale is easier to commit to when you have a plan for the money and the time.
- **Tax and legal effects.** These depend on your country and your deal. Ask an accountant and an attorney before you decide.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I test the market before I decide?

Yes. A free valuation shows a likely range, and listing does not oblige you to accept an offer. See [how to sell a website](/guides/how-to-sell-a-website/).

### Can I make it worth more first?

Often. Cleaner records, fewer owner hours and a more varied traffic mix tend to raise what buyers will pay. See [increase website value before selling](/guides/increase-website-value-before-selling/).
