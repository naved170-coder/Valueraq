## Key takeaway

Acquire.com is an established marketplace that charges sellers a monthly fee and a percentage at closing, and charges buyers for access to sellers. In exchange it provides an established buyer base, legal document tools and escrow at closing. {{BRAND}} is new: it launched in 2026, has few listings and no track record of completed sales. It is free to list on and takes no commission, and it leaves the deal and the payment to the buyer and seller. For reach, the established marketplace has a clear advantage.

{{BRAND}} is not affiliated with, endorsed by or partnered with Acquire.com. "Acquire.com" and "MicroAcquire" are trademarks of their owner and are used here only to identify the service.

## When Acquire.com is the better choice

- **You are selling a SaaS business or startup and want buyers who are looking for exactly that.** Acquire.com has built its audience around startup acquisitions, and it has far more buyers than a marketplace that opened this year.
- **You want escrow built into the closing.** Acquire.com closes deals through Escrow.com or a partner law firm. On {{BRAND}} you choose and arrange an escrow service yourself.
- **You want deal tools and guidance.** Acquire.com provides automated NDAs and legal document builders, and offers an adviser-led service, "Guided by Acquire", to SaaS businesses with $100,000 or more in annual revenue. {{BRAND}} is not a broker and gives no representation in a deal.
- **A track record matters to you or your buyer.** Acquire.com has completed acquisitions behind it. {{BRAND}} has none yet.

## When {{BRAND}} is the better choice

- **You want a free valuation and want to see how it was worked out.** The [valuation tools](/tools/) cover six business types, and the formulas and default multiples are published in the [methodology](/methodology/). The multiples are {{BRAND}}'s own editorial assumptions, not figures derived from a transaction dataset; see [data sources](/data-sources/).
- **You want to list without paying.** Listing is free and there is no closing fee, commission or success fee, so nothing is deducted from the price by {{BRAND}}. See [fees](/fees/).
- **You are happy to run the deal yourself.** You talk to buyers through private messaging with file attachments, agree terms directly and arrange your own escrow and sale agreement. [How it works](/how-it-works/) sets out the steps.

The trade-off is plain: no fees, but a much smaller audience and more of the work and the safeguards left to you. Read [buyer safety](/buyer-safety/) before sending or accepting money.

## Can you use both?

Often yes, but check the terms first. A seller can generally list a business in more than one place unless they have signed an exclusivity agreement. Read Acquire.com's current terms, and any broker or advisory agreement you have signed, before listing the same business elsewhere. Acquire.com's help centre also says its closing fee is still due if a deal that began on its platform closes off it.

A practical way to combine them is to use the {{BRAND}} tools to work out a realistic price range, then list wherever suits the size and type of your business.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is VALUERAQ a replacement for Acquire.com?

No. They overlap but are not equivalent. Acquire.com is an established marketplace with a large buyer base, deal tools and escrow at closing. {{BRAND}} is a new marketplace with few listings, built around free valuation tools and free listings, and it does not handle payment or act as a broker.

### Which costs less to sell on?

{{BRAND}} charges nothing to list and no commission. Acquire.com charges a monthly listing fee of $25 to $100 and a closing fee of 6% to 8% if you sell. Cost is only one factor: a larger audience can produce more offers, and on a free listing you still pay for escrow and any advice.

### Can I get a refund on an Acquire.com subscription?

Acquire.com's subscription terms say buyer subscriptions are not refunded after seven days and are not prorated on cancellation, and that seller listing fees are not refunded except as those terms describe. Read the current terms before paying. On {{BRAND}} there is nothing to refund for a listing, because listing is free.
